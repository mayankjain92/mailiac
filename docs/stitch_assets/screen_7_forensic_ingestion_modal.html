EXPORTING .EML (GMAIL)

  1. 01. Open the target email in Gmail.
  2. 02. Click the three-dot menu (More) in the top right.
  3. 03. Select 'Show original' from the dropdown.
  4. 04. Click 'Download Original' in the new tab.
